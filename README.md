# Research Craft

Evidence retrieval, comparison, synthesis and verification.

This is an independent, project-neutral skill. Its entry point is [SKILL.md](skills/research-craft/SKILL.md). Install the complete `skills/research-craft` directory, including its references. It can be used directly; no ECD studio, producer or other department is a prerequisite.

Project objectives, audiences, styles, formats, research conclusions, operational targets and permissions belong to the project and are supplied per task. They must not become defaults in this skill or its supporting files.

This independent capability remains an alpha. The methods cover question decomposition, traceable searching, evidence provenance, result-level comparison, sampling and transfer limits, claim verification, and material-use conditions. Domain-specific frameworks retain their own scope; this skill does not certify a systematic review or replace a complete statistical, experimental, or qualitative method.

Structural validation and limited forward tasks establish initial evidence, not comprehensive mastery or a measured improvement across all research uses. Full maintenance research and case records remain outside the installable package.
